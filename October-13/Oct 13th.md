**Description**  
The pricing and offer decisions an aesthetic practice makes in October set patient expectations well beyond the season. Packages built under competitive pressure, discounts introduced to fill a quiet week, and promotions copied from a competitor all carry forward into the following year as the price patients believe is normal. This panel brings financial discipline to year-end pricing, showing owners how to build offers that lift revenue without training patients to wait.

**Key Discussion Points include:**

* **The long-term impact of year-end pricing decisions on patient expectations and practice profitability**  
* **The margin mathematics behind seasonal offers and promotions**  
* **Profitable alternatives to year-end discounting**  
* **Protecting pricing integrity when competitors are cutting prices**
